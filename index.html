More Than A Shirt

More Than A Shirt

Every design begins with a real Cape Cod place — not a stock photo, not a generic outline of a beach chair. We visit, we sketch, we paint, and only then do we call a design finished.

The artwork preserves the real landscape while capturing the feeling of being there: the light on Scargo Lake, the walk out to The Knob, the marsh grass bending at low tide. It's a small, growing collection meant to be recognized, not just worn — the kind of thing you hold onto.

2026 Summer Contest Rules

By submitting an entry, you agree to the following rules. Questions may be sent to Contact@GraniteSW.com.

  1. No Purchase Necessary

    No purchase or payment is required to enter or win. A purchase will not improve an entrant's chances of being selected.

  2. Eligibility

    The contest is open to legal residents of the United States who are 18 years of age or older when they enter. Employees, contractors, and immediate family or household members of Cape Cod Vibin' or GraniteSW are not eligible. Void where prohibited by law.

  3. Contest Period

    The contest begins July 19, 2026 at 12:00 a.m. Eastern Time and ends Monday, September 7, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. Entries received after the deadline are not eligible.

  4. How to Enter

    Submit the official Google entry form with the requested information. Limit one entry per person. Duplicate, incomplete, automated, or fraudulent entries may be disqualified.

  5. Submission Requirements

    Each entry must nominate one genuine Cape Cod location and must not contain unlawful, offensive, defamatory, or infringing material. Entrants represent that they have the right to submit any original words or images included with the entry.

  6. Selection

    Cape Cod Vibin' will select one winning location based on its Cape Cod relevance, the story shared, visual potential, distinctiveness, and fit with the existing collection. This is a judged contest, not a random drawing. All decisions are final.

  7. Prize

    One winner will receive one Cape Cod Vibin' T-shirt featuring the design inspired by the winning location, with standard U.S. shipping included. Size and color are subject to availability. The prize is non-transferable and cannot be redeemed for cash.

  8. Winner Notification

    The potential winner will be contacted using the information provided on the form and must respond within seven calendar days. They may be asked to confirm eligibility and provide a shirt size and shipping address. If they cannot be reached or are ineligible, an alternate winner may be selected.

  9. Permission to Develop the Design

    By entering, each entrant gives Cape Cod Vibin' permission to use, adapt, illustrate, publish, promote, and commercially develop the submitted location idea and any accompanying entry materials without additional payment or royalties beyond the stated prize.

  10. Privacy

    Entry information will be used to administer the contest, contact the winner, and fulfill the prize. Shipping information will be requested only from the selected winner. Entries are submitted through Google Forms and are also subject to Google's applicable privacy practices.

  11. General Conditions

    Cape Cod Vibin' may disqualify entries that violate these rules and may suspend, modify, or cancel the contest if fraud, technical failure, or circumstances beyond its reasonable control compromise the contest. Entrants are responsible for any taxes associated with accepting a prize.

  12. Sponsor and Platform Disclaimer

    The contest is sponsored by Cape Cod Vibin'. Google and Printify are not sponsors, administrators, or endorsers of this contest and are released from responsibility related to its administration.
